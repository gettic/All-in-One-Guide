Third-party content
-------------------

Fonts (media\fonts\, SIL Open Font License 1.1; the licence and the
copyright notices are in media\fonts\OFL.txt; the fonts stay under the OFL):
- OpenSans.ttf: a subset of Open Sans SemiBold, Copyright 2020 The Open Sans
  Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/opensans).
- AOGReadable.ttf ("Easy-read"): a renamed subset of OpenDyslexic Regular by
  Abbie Gonzalez (https://github.com/antijingoist/opendyslexic). OpenDyslexic
  is a Reserved Font Name, so our changed copy has its own name.
Both are built by tools\build_fonts.py (in the source repository).

Warcraft Wiki (https://warcraft.wiki.gg), by the Warcraft Wiki contributors.
Text licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Used: single facts (ids, coordinates, levels) in the guide routes.
Changes: the facts are restated in our own words; no wiki text or images
are copied. Facts from the wiki are marked "wiki" in the guide pack sources.

Questie and QuestieDB (https://github.com/Questie/Questie), GPL-3.0, by
the Questie contributors. Used: quest givers, turn-ins, levels, chains,
objectives and NPC spots, as facts for the routes and their tests.

CMaNGOS classic-db (https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db), GPL-3.0.
Some NPC positions, quest giver/turn-in ids, creature spawn areas
(grid-snapped), city gate object positions (smart arrow), quest item
and loot/vendor ids, model display ids, mob names (target button), item names
(item pictures), quest levels
(quest giver check), and
spawn spots and respawn times were checked against or derived
from this database. Game facts in it come from World of Warcraft,
(c) Blizzard Entertainment; they are not covered by our GPL licence
and are used here only as reference facts in a free, non-commercial
addon. Not affiliated with or endorsed by CMaNGOS or Blizzard.

Shortest Path Forever (https://github.com/cjber/shortest-path-forever),
GPL-3.0-or-later, by Cillian Berragan. Used: positions read off its baked
walking grid (the floors, heights and steps of the Deathknell start crypt)
for the smart arrow's stairs points; read with its own decoder, no code or
files copied. Marked "spf" in the connector sources.

Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net): some quest ids, levels,
XP, prerequisites, NPC ids and quest-item ids for WoW Forever-only quests,
used as reference facts. ForeverDB states no licence for its data; no
texts were copied. Not affiliated with ForeverDB or Blizzard.
